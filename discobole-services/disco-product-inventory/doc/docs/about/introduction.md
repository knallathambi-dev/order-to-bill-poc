# Introduction

## What & Why

!!! abstract "What is Product Inventory?"
    Product Inventory is the responsibility of OTB (Order To Bill) domain and represents a central module in an operational Core Commerce IS ecosystem. It is destinated to telco service provider entending to offer an ODA/TMF compliant component that the key principle is to maintain the association between customers and purchased product instances.

## History & Perspectives

!!! info "History And Perspectives"
    - The development of the [Product Inventory](https://gitlab.ow2.org/discobole/disco-oda-components/disco-product-inventory) component was initiated in the context of the [DISCOBOLE](https://discobole.ow2.io/doc/) project that the aim is to develop an agile & disruptive IT Product (suite of OpenSource components) on the scope Order To Bill & Customer Order Orchestration and Delivery (COOD).
    - This suite of OpenSource components are based on TM Forum ODA component definition, implements the TMF Forum APIs and is based on TMF SID data model while complying with Orange’s functional architecture principles.
    - The functional use case that [Product Inventory](https://gitlab.ow2.org/discobole/disco-oda-components/disco-product-inventory) implements is the storage and exposure of products that are assigned to and used by Parties.
